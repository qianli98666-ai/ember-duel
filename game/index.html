EMBER DUEL

A turn-based card duel of champions, guards and spells.

How to play

  • You and the foe start with 30 life. First to fall loses.
  • Each turn your ember refills and grows by 1 (up to 8). Spend ember to play cards.
  • Champions stay on your side and strike the foe when you end your turn.
  • Guards raise a shield that soaks up the foe's strike before it reaches your life.
  • Spells burn, mend or draw cards at once.
  • Click a card (or press 1-7) to play it. Press Enter to end your turn.
